Warm and still swimmable; nights near 24 °C. Showers pass quickly, but wind is the real threat on an exposed pitch. October is still typhoon season — roughly a 1-in-10 chance of one within 300 km over three days, so keep a wet-weather fallback.

Driving
  • Expressway, Naha IC ↔ Kyoda IC57 km · ¥1,040 by ETC, ¥1,610 cash — the discount is ETC-only
  • Route 58 is the scenic alternativeRoughly twice as long to Nago
  • Drive on the left · watch the bus-only lanes in central Naha at rush hour
Hazards worth taking seriously
  • Habu vipers — nocturnal, high-risk seasonTorch after dark, closed shoes, stay off stone walls and long grass. Keep 1.5 m back. If bitten: stay still, call 119, no tourniquet, no cutting
  • Jellyfish — assume no nets, no lifeguardGorilla Chop and Maeda are unpatrolled. A long-sleeve rash guard beats any first aid
  • Stings: rinse with seawater, never fresh waterVinegar is only for habu-kurage. On a Portuguese man o' war — blue float, long blue threads — it makes things worse. Lift tentacles off with pincers, then cool
  • Centipedes get into boots and tentsShake out footwear, zip the tent
  • No open fires or charcoal on sandMost sites require a fire pan; the set's grill sidesteps it
Practical
  • Tap water is safe to drinkCarry 5–10 L anyway — wild sites often have no tap
  • Supermarkets north of Nago shut by ~19:00Kunigami and Ogimi have none at all — only 24 h konbini on Route 58
  • Carry cash — the best soba shops are cash-only
  • Mon Oct 12 is Sports DayThree-day weekend: book campsites early
