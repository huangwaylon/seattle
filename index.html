Swimmable, with nights near 24 °C. Showers are brief; wind is the main risk on an exposed pitch. October is typhoon season — about a 1-in-10 chance of one within 300 km over three days.

Driving
  • Expressway, Naha IC ↔ Kyoda IC57 km · ¥1,040 ETC, ¥1,610 cash · discount is ETC-only
  • Route 58, the coastal alternativeAbout twice as long to Nago
  • Drive on the left · bus-only lanes in central Naha at rush hour
Hazards
  • Habu vipers — nocturnal, active Sep–NovTorch after dark, closed shoes, avoid stone walls and long grass. Strike range 1.5 m. If bitten: stay still, call 119, no tourniquet, no cutting
  • Jellyfish — no nets, no lifeguardGorilla Chop and Maeda are unpatrolled. Long-sleeve rash guard
  • Stings: rinse with seawater, never fresh waterVinegar for habu-kurage only. On a Portuguese man o' war — blue float, long blue threads — vinegar worsens it. Remove tentacles with pincers, then cool
  • Centipedes enter boots and tentsShake out footwear, keep the tent zipped
  • No open fires or charcoal on sandMost sites require a fire pan
Practical
  • Tap water is potableCarry 5–10 L — wild sites often have no tap
  • Supermarkets north of Nago close by ~19:00None in Kunigami or Ogimi · 24 h konbini on Route 58
  • Many soba shops are cash-only
  • Mon Oct 12 is Sports DayThree-day weekend · book campsites ahead
