Still properly warm and swimmable, with nights staying near 24 °C — the rental's bedding is plenty. Showers blow through quickly. Wind is the real threat on an exposed pitch, and it will also make a cassette burner useless, so pick somewhere sheltered. October is still typhoon season: roughly a 1-in-10 chance of one tracking within 300 km during a three-day window, so keep a wet-weather fallback in mind.

Driving
  • Okinawa Expressway, Naha IC ↔ Kyoda IC57 km · ¥1,040 with an ETC card against ¥1,610 in cash — the 35% discount is ETC-only now
  • Route 58 is the slow, scenic alternativeNaha to Nago takes roughly twice as long
  • Drive on the left · no turn on red · watch the bus-only lanes in central Naha at rush hour
Hazards worth taking seriously
  • Habu vipers — high-risk season, nocturnalThe prefecture's campaign runs Sep–Nov and they do not hibernate. Torch after dark, closed shoes, stay off stone walls and long grass. Keep 1.5 m back — they cannot jump. If bitten: keep still, call 119, no tourniquet and no cutting
  • Jellyfish — assume no nets and no lifeguardThe box-jellyfish advisory runs Jun–Sep, so nets are likely already down, and Gorilla Chop and Maeda are unpatrolled anyway. A long-sleeve rash guard beats any first aid
  • Stings: rinse with seawater, never fresh waterVinegar is only for habu-kurage (cube-shaped bell, pale tentacles). On a Portuguese man o' war — blue float, long blue threads — vinegar makes it worse. Lift tentacles off, then cool it
  • Centipedes get into boots and tentsShake out footwear, zip the tent, don't pitch on leaf litter
  • No open fires or charcoal on sandOnna Village asks visitors to use neither, and most sites require a fire pan. A gas burner sidesteps all of it
Practical
  • Tap water is safe to drinkHard in the centre and south, softer up north off the Yanbaru dams. Carry 5–10 L anyway — wild sites often have no tap
  • Supermarkets north of Nago shut by ~19:00Kunigami and Ogimi have no chain supermarket at all — only 24 h konbini on Route 58 after that
  • Carry cash — the best soba shops and Union supermarkets are cash-only
  • Mon Oct 12 is Sports DayA three-day weekend: book campsites early, expect crowds
